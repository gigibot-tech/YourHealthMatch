# YourHealthMatch positioning

Working source for the website, outreach, and partnerships.

The grant source is the first Ideenpapier, `YourHealthMatch_Ideenpapier_EXIST.docx` (not the later EXIST2 file). It is confidential and stays outside this repository. This note keeps only the public lines the site should repeat.

## Who it is for

International students, expats, and newly arrived workers in Germany. The first region in the paper is Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, with the demo in Greifswald.

People who grew up in Germany also struggle to reach the right specialist. They can still navigate because the system is in their language. Newcomers face that same search, plus a new system, new insurance rules, and a desk that may not speak their language.

Clinics are the supply side: multilingual practices that publish the times they can actually offer.

## The problem

The paper’s point is orientation, not a longer directory. Healthcare navigation in Germany does not guarantee the right next step. A listing, a hotline, or a tip from a friend does not say which specialty comes first, who speaks your language, who accepts your insurance, and who has the right time.

## What we do

YourHealthMatch is a medical access layer. Phase 1 matching stays a clear sequence: language, specialty, insurance type, then a time in the app or a route to the platform where that doctor already books. Results stay short, about three to five doctors. We do not sync Doctolib, Jameda, or similar calendars.

The first action on the site is finding a suitable doctor. Joining the pilot list is a second action. Trust is meant to come through institutions people already know, such as a university international office or a welcome center. A partner mark appears only after that cooperation exists.

## What we do not claim

- We are not a medical practice and not emergency care.
- We do not show university or partner marks until an agreement exists.
- Germany, starting with a Greifswald demo in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, is the first pilot. It is not a national network.

## Product assumptions

- Matching stays a closed set of rules. A phrase may only point at a known lead. It does not invent a specialty.
- Public insurance matches only a practice with KV approval. Private insurance and self-pay are separate.
- An in-person visit stays near the person. A video visit does not use distance.
- Each patient session keeps its own profile in the browser. A later session does not overwrite the earlier one.
- After a booking, that doctor stays with the specialty so the person can return.
